Kedi Kafe

Serve orders, grow your cafe.

Grow a little cat cafe. Drag two identical items onto each other to reveal the next one. Complete five-level chains, keep up with customer orders, and spend your coins on the journey ahead.

Features

  • 38 categories, five levels each
  • 10,000 levels, each with its own story and reward
  • Travel the journey map stop by stop
  • Complete your collection
  • Playable offline
